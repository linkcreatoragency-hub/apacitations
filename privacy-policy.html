Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how APACitations.com ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and citation tools.

Information we collect

Information you provide: The source details you type into the citation generator (titles, author names, URLs) are processed in your browser to build your citation. We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name or email to use the tool.

Automatically collected information: Like most websites, we and our service providers may automatically collect standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and referring pages. This is used for site analytics, performance, and security.

Autocite requests: When you use the URL autocite feature, the address you paste is sent to our server so we can fetch that page's public metadata (title, author, date). These requests may be cached temporarily to improve performance and are not linked to you personally.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use them, and allow certain third parties to use them, in order to operate the site, understand how it is used, and serve advertising. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings; the citation tools work with cookies disabled.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

We display advertisements served by Google AdSense to keep this site free.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of many third-party vendors' cookies at www.aboutads.info.

For more information on how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics services to understand aggregate usage of the site (e.g., which guides are most read). This data is not used to identify individuals.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers necessary to run the site (hosting, analytics, advertising as described above) or when required by law.

Children's privacy

Our website is a general-audience educational tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on your location (including under GDPR and CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, and to opt out of certain data uses. To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@apacitations.com.

Links to other sites

Our site and generated citations contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@apacitations.com or via our contact page.
